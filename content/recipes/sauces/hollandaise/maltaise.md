Hollandaise with blood orange in place of the lemon. Served with asparagus, and
it is worth waiting for blood orange season rather than using ordinary orange.

## Ingredients

### Added to the base

- Juice of 1 blood orange, reduced to 2 tbsp
- Zest of 1 blood orange

## Method

1. Reduce the blood orange juice to about 2 tablespoons and let it cool.
2. Make the hollandaise as usual, using the reduced juice in place of the lemon.
3. Stir the zest through at the end.

## Notes

- Reduce the juice first. Orange is less acidic and much wetter than lemon, so
  unreduced it slackens the sauce.
