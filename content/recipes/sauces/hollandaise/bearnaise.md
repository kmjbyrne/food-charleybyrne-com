The classic steak sauce. It is the same emulsion as hollandaise, but the acid
comes from a reduction of vinegar, wine, shallot and tarragon instead of lemon
juice.

## Ingredients

### Reduction

- 3 tbsp white wine vinegar
- 3 tbsp dry white wine
- 1 shallot, finely chopped
- 2 tbsp tarragon stalks, chopped
- 6 black peppercorns, cracked

### Sauce

- 2 tbsp fresh tarragon leaves, chopped

## Method

1. Simmer the vinegar, wine, shallot, tarragon stalks and peppercorns until
   reduced to about 2 tablespoons. Strain and cool slightly.
2. Whisk the hollandaise yolks with the reduction in a bowl over barely
   simmering water until thick enough to leave a ribbon. The reduction replaces
   the lemon juice.
3. Off the heat, add the warm butter in a thin stream, whisking constantly.
4. Stir in the tarragon leaves and season.

## Notes

- Give the reduction time. Rushed, it leaves the sauce sharp and raw tasting.
- Split sauce is rescued by whisking it slowly into a fresh yolk.
