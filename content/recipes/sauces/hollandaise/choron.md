Bearnaise with reduced tomato folded through. It comes out coral pink and a
little sharper than bearnaise alone.

## Ingredients

### Added to the base

- 3 tbsp tomato purée, or 2 tomatoes reduced to a paste
- 2 tbsp tarragon and shallot reduction, as for bearnaise

## Method

1. Make the bearnaise reduction and the emulsion as usual.
2. Cook the tomato purée gently 5 minutes to drive off its raw edge and any
   water. It should be thick enough to hold its shape.
3. Fold the cooled tomato into the finished sauce a spoonful at a time.
4. Check the seasoning. Tomato flattens salt, so it usually needs more.

## Notes

- Wet tomato splits the emulsion. Reduce it properly and let it cool before it
  goes in.
- Traditionally served with tournedos and grilled fish.
