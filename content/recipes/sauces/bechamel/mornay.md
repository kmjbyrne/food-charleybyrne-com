Bechamel with cheese and a little egg yolk stirred through. Use it for croque
monsieur, gratins and anything you want to brown under a grill.

## Ingredients

- 60g gruyere, grated
- 30g parmesan, grated
- 1 egg yolk, optional
- Pinch of nutmeg
- Salt and white pepper

## Method

1. Bring the bechamel to a bare simmer. It must not boil once the cheese is in.
2. Take it off the heat and stir in the gruyere and parmesan a handful at a
   time, letting each melt before the next.
3. If using, beat a spoonful of the warm sauce into the yolk, then stir that
   back in. This is what gives it gloss and helps it glaze.
4. Season with nutmeg, salt and white pepper.

## Notes

- Do not boil it once the cheese is in. The proteins tighten and the fat
  separates out.
- Gruyere melts smooth, parmesan brings salt and savour. Cheddar works but goes
  greasier.
