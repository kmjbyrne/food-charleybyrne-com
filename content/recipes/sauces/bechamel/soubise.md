Onion cooked until it surrenders completely, then folded through bechamel and
passed. Sweet, pale and very smooth. Good with lamb, eggs and white fish.

## Ingredients

- 500ml bechamel, warm
- 2 large onions, finely sliced
- 30g butter
- 50ml cream, optional
- Salt and white pepper

## Method

1. Melt the butter over low heat. Add the onions with a pinch of salt.
2. Cover and sweat 25 to 30 minutes, stirring now and then. They must stay pale,
   so drop the heat if they start to colour.
3. Stir the onions into the warm bechamel and simmer 10 minutes.
4. Blend smooth, then pass through a fine sieve.
5. Stir in the cream if using. Season.

## Notes

- Colour is the enemy here. Browned onion makes it taste like French onion soup
  and turns the sauce beige.
