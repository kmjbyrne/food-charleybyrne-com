Butter, flour and milk in a ratio of 1:1:8 by volume. Two tablespoons each of
butter and flour to one cup of milk.

The keto version reaches the same place without a roux, using cream cheese to
thicken instead of flour. It is richer and does not hold as well on a plate, but
it behaves the same in a lasagne or a croque monsieur.

## Ingredients

### Classic

- 35g (1/4 cup) unsalted butter
- 30g (1/3 cup) plain flour
- 600ml (2 1/2 cups) milk
- 1/4 tsp ground nutmeg
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 tsp salt and white pepper

### Keto

- 2 tbsp butter
- 1/3 cup cream cheese
- 1 cup double cream
- Pinch of ground nutmeg
- Salt and pepper

## Method

### Classic Method

1. Melt the butter in a heavy pan over medium-low heat.
2. Stir in the flour and cook 2 to 3 minutes to a white roux. It should bubble
   and smell biscuity but stay pale.
3. Add the milk a ladle at a time, whisking each addition smooth before the
   next.
4. Add the bay leaf and simmer gently 10 to 15 minutes, stirring often, until it
   coats a spoon.
5. Remove the bay leaf. Season with nutmeg, salt and white pepper.

### Keto Method

1. Melt the butter and cream cheese together in a small pan over medium heat,
   whisking until smooth.
2. Pour in the cream, whisking it evenly through.
3. Bring to a simmer.
4. Add the seasonings and take it off the heat.
5. Let it cool a little. It thickens as it stands.

## Derivatives

Mornay adds gruyere and parmesan. Soubise adds sweated onion, passed smooth.
Nantua adds crayfish butter and cream. Cheddar cheese sauce is the domestic
cousin of Mornay.

## Notes

- Cold milk into a hot roux is the reliable way to avoid lumps. If both are hot,
  it seizes.
- Keep the roux pale. Any colour and it stops being a bechamel.
- The keto version cannot be made ahead as confidently. It splits if you reheat
  it hard, so warm it gently.
