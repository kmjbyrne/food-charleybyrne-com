The shellfish bechamel. Crayfish butter carries both the colour and the flavour,
so the butter is where the work goes. Classically served with quenelles de
brochet.

## Ingredients

### Added to the base

- 100g crayfish or prawn shells
- 80g butter
- 100ml double cream
- 1 tbsp brandy, optional
- Pinch of cayenne

## Method

1. Dry the shells in a low oven 10 minutes, then pound or blitz them coarsely.
2. Melt the butter with the shells over low heat 20 minutes without colouring.
   Strain through muslin and chill until firm.
3. Warm the bechamel and stir in the cream. Simmer 5 minutes.
4. Off the heat, whisk in the crayfish butter a piece at a time.
5. Add the brandy if using, and season with cayenne and salt.

## Notes

- The butter must go in off the heat. Boiling it out undoes the emulsion and the
  colour goes flat.
- Prawn shells work if crayfish are not available, though the colour is paler.
