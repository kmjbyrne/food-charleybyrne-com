Escoffier's tomato sauce is not Italian tomato sauce. It starts with salt pork
and mirepoix, includes stock, and is traditionally thickened with a light roux.

## Ingredients

- 60g salt pork or pancetta, diced
- 1 small onion, diced
- 1 carrot, diced
- 30g plain flour
- 1kg ripe tomatoes, chopped, or 800g tinned
- 500ml white stock, warm
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 tsp sugar
- Salt and pepper

## Method

1. Render the salt pork gently until the fat runs and the pieces colour.
2. Add the onion and carrot and cook until soft and lightly golden.
3. Stir in the flour and cook 2 to 3 minutes.
4. Add the tomatoes, stock, bay leaf and sugar. Bring to a simmer.
5. Cook gently 1.5 hours, uncovered, stirring now and then.
6. Pass through a fine sieve or a food mill. Season.

## Notes

- The sugar corrects acidity. Taste before you add it, since ripe summer
  tomatoes rarely need any.
- Pass it through a sieve or a food mill. The finished sauce should be smooth.

## Derivatives

Creole adds peppers and celery. Portugaise adds garlic and diced tomato flesh.
Provencale leans on garlic, olive oil and herbs.
