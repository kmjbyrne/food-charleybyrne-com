Sauce tomat with the vegetables left in rather than passed out. Chunkier than
its mother and served with rice, eggs and white fish.

## Ingredients

### Added to the base

- 1 green pepper, diced
- 2 sticks celery, diced
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 1 bay leaf
- Pinch of cayenne
- 1 tsp thyme leaves

## Method

1. Sweat the pepper, celery and garlic in a little butter until soft.
2. Add the sauce tomat, bay leaf and thyme.
3. Simmer 20 minutes so the vegetables take on the sauce.
4. Season with cayenne and salt.

## Notes

- Do not pass this one. The point is the texture the vegetables give it.
