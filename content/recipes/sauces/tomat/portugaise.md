Sauce tomat with fresh tomato concassé folded in at the end, so it keeps some
bite against the smooth base.

## Ingredients

### Added to the base

- 3 tomatoes, peeled, seeded and diced
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp parsley, chopped

## Method

1. Cook the onion and garlic in the olive oil until soft, without colouring.
2. Add the diced tomato and cook 5 minutes, just to soften.
3. Stir this into the warm sauce tomat.
4. Finish with parsley off the heat.

## Notes

- Peel and seed the tomatoes properly. Skins curl into unpleasant strips and the
  seeds make it watery.
