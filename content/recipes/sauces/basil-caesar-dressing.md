## Ingredients

- 2 minced garlic cloves.
- 2 tsp basil or chopped fresh leaves.
- 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce.
- 3/4 cup mayonnaise.
- 1 tsp lemon juice.
- 1/3 tsp pepper.
- pinch of salt.
- 1 tsp mustard.
- Anchovy, to taste. Any of these work: 1 tsp anchovy paste, 2 or 3 oil-packed
  fillets minced, or 1 tsp fish sauce. Leave it out for a vegetarian dressing.

## Method

Put all ingredients into a food processor or blender and pulse until there are
no visible large bits of garlic or basil.

## Notes

The anchovy is what makes it taste like Caesar dressing rather than garlic
mayonnaise. It does not read as fish, it reads as savoury depth. Paste is the
easiest to dose, whole fillets give the roundest flavour, and fish sauce is the
sharpest of the three, so start at half and taste.
