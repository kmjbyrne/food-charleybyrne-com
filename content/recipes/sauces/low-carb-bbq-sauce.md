## Ingredients

### Base

- 1 1/2 cups passata, unsweetened
- 1/4 cup apple cider vinegar
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 1/2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 2 tbsp Dijon mustard, or yellow mustard
- 1 tsp liquid smoke
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/2 tsp chilli powder
- 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper, optional
- 1/2 tsp ground black pepper
- 1/2 to 1 tsp salt, to taste
- 1 tbsp soy sauce or coconut aminos, optional, for umami
- 1 to 2 tsp unsweetened cocoa powder

### Keto sweetener

- 1/4 cup allulose or erythritol
- Pinch of pure stevia
- 1/4 tsp xanthan gum, optional, for thickening

### Standard sweetener

- 1/4 cup brown sugar

## Method

1. Whisk the passata, vinegar, lemon juice, Worcestershire sauce and mustard
   together in a saucepan until smooth.
2. Stir in the garlic powder, onion powder, smoked paprika, chilli powder,
   cayenne, black pepper, salt and soy sauce.
3. Add the cocoa powder a teaspoon at a time, whisking it in fully before adding
   more. It should read as depth, not chocolate.
4. Stir in your sweetener and the liquid smoke.
5. Bring to a bare simmer over medium-low heat, then cook 20 minutes uncovered,
   stirring every few minutes. It will darken and thicken.
6. Taste and adjust the salt, vinegar and sweetener. Blend smooth with a stick
   blender if you want it glossy.
7. Cool before bottling. It thickens further in the fridge.

## Notes

- Allulose is the better keto choice. It caramelises like sugar and does not
  recrystallise in the fridge, which erythritol will.
- If you use erythritol, add a pinch of pure stevia alongside it. Erythritol has
  a cooling effect on the palate that reads as menthol in a warm sauce, and the
  stevia offsets it. A very small amount goes a long way.
- The cocoa is not there to taste of chocolate. It supplies the bitterness and
  body that molasses gives a conventional sauce. Start at 1 tsp.
- Only add the xanthan gum once the sauce is off the heat and you have judged
  the consistency. Sprinkle it in while blending or it clumps.
- Keeps two weeks refrigerated in a sealed jar.
