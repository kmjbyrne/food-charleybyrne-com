Made in the pan the steak came out of, so all the fond goes into it. The glace does the work that a long reduction would otherwise have to.

## Ingredients

:sub-recipe{path="/recipes/stocks/glace-de-viande" section="Ingredients" label="Glace de viande, made ahead"}

- 2 shallots, finely chopped
- 30g butter
- 1 to 2 cubes glace de viande
- 1 tbsp black peppercorns, coarsely crushed
- 60ml brandy
- 1 clove garlic, minced, optional
- 100ml double cream
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- Salt

## Method

1. Pour off most of the fat from the steak pan, leaving the browned bits.
2. Soften the shallots in the butter over medium heat, scraping the fond as they go.
3. Add the crushed peppercorns and plenty of salt. Be heavier with the pepper than feels sensible; the cream mutes it.
4. Off the heat, add the brandy and flame it. Once the flames die, return to the heat and reduce until nearly dry.
5. Add the garlic if using and cook 30 seconds.
6. Drop in the glace cubes and let them melt into the pan.
7. Stir in the cream and simmer 2 to 3 minutes until it coats a spoon.
8. Take it off the heat and whisk in the mustard. Taste for salt.

## Notes

- Crush the peppercorns coarsely in a mortar. Ground pepper turns the sauce gritty and muddy brown.
- Mustard goes in off the heat. Boiled, it splits and turns bitter.
- Without glace, use 150ml strong beef stock and reduce it hard before the cream. It will not have the same body.
