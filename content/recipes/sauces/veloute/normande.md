Built on fish velouté rather than chicken, with mushroom cooking liquor and
often the liquor from steamed mussels. The classic sauce for sole normande.

## Ingredients

### Added to the base

- 150ml mushroom cooking liquor
- 100ml mussel or oyster liquor, optional
- 2 egg yolks
- 100ml double cream
- 30g cold butter

## Method

1. Use a fish velouté as the base.
2. Add the mushroom liquor and any shellfish liquor. Reduce by a third.
3. Beat the yolks with the cream, temper with a ladle of the hot sauce, then
   return it to the pan.
4. Cook gently until it coats a spoon, without boiling.
5. Whisk in the cold butter off the heat.

## Notes

- Shellfish liquor is salty. Season only at the end.
