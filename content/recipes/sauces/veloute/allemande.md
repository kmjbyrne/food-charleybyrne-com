Velouté finished with a liaison of yolk and cream. Escoffier reclassified it as
a daughter rather than a mother, which is how it is usually taught now.

## Ingredients

### Added to the base

- 2 egg yolks
- 100ml double cream
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 30g cold butter

## Method

1. Bring the velouté to a bare simmer and reduce slightly.
2. Beat the yolks with the cream in a bowl.
3. Whisk a ladle of the hot velouté into the yolks to temper them, then pour
   that back into the pan, whisking.
4. Cook over low heat until it thickens. Do not let it boil or the yolks
   scramble.
5. Off the heat, add the lemon juice and whisk in the cold butter.

## Notes

- Tempering is not optional. Yolks added straight to hot sauce curdle.
- It will not hold at a simmer. Make it close to serving.
