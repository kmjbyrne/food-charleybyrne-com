Chicken velouté reduced with mushroom trimmings, then finished with cream and
butter. Pale, glossy and rich. The classic partner to poached chicken.

## Ingredients

- 500ml chicken velouté
- 100g mushroom trimmings or stalks
- 150ml double cream
- 30g cold butter, cubed
- Squeeze of lemon
- Salt and white pepper

## Method

1. Simmer the velouté with the mushroom trimmings 15 minutes to draw out their
   flavour.
2. Strain through a fine sieve, pressing lightly.
3. Return to the heat and reduce by about a quarter.
4. Stir in the cream and simmer 5 minutes until it coats a spoon.
5. Off the heat, whisk in the cold butter a cube at a time.
6. Finish with lemon, salt and white pepper.

## Notes

- The butter goes in off the heat and cold. Hot butter will not emulsify and the
  sauce turns oily.
