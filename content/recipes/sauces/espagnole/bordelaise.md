Red wine and shallot reduced hard, then let down with espagnole and finished
with poached marrow. The traditional sauce for a grilled steak.

## Ingredients

- 300ml dry red wine
- 2 shallots, finely chopped
- 1 sprig thyme, 1 bay leaf
- 400ml espagnole or demi-glace
- 60g beef marrow, diced
- 30g cold butter
- Salt and pepper

## Method

1. Simmer the wine, shallots, thyme and bay until reduced to about a quarter.
2. Add the espagnole and simmer gently 20 minutes, skimming.
3. Poach the marrow in barely simmering salted water 2 minutes, then drain.
4. Strain the sauce, return to the heat and whisk in the cold butter off the
   boil.
5. Add the marrow and season. Do not boil it again.

## Notes

- Marrow is traditional and worth it, but the sauce works without. Add a little
  more butter at the end if you skip it.
