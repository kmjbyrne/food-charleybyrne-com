Mushrooms and shallots cooked hard, deglazed with white wine and brandy, then
let down with espagnole and a little tomato. Served with chicken and rabbit.

## Ingredients

### Added to the base

- 200g button mushrooms, sliced
- 2 shallots, finely chopped
- 30g butter
- 100ml dry white wine
- 1 tbsp brandy, optional
- 1 tbsp tomato purée
- 1 tbsp tarragon and parsley, chopped

## Method

1. Colour the mushrooms hard in the butter. Crowding steams them, so use a wide
   pan and work in batches if you need to.
2. Add the shallots and cook 2 minutes.
3. Deglaze with the wine and brandy, and reduce by half.
4. Stir in the tomato purée and cook 1 minute.
5. Add the espagnole and simmer 15 minutes.
6. Finish with the chopped herbs off the heat.

## Notes

- The herbs go in at the end. Cooked into the sauce they turn dull and grassy.
