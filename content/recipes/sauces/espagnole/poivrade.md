The classical pepper sauce. Mirepoix and vinegar reduced hard, espagnole added,
crushed peppercorns steeped in at the end so they stay aromatic rather than
turning acrid.

## Ingredients

### Added to the base

- 1 carrot, diced small
- 1 shallot, diced small
- 30g butter
- 100ml red wine vinegar
- 150ml red wine
- 1 tbsp black peppercorns, coarsely crushed
- 1 sprig thyme, 1 bay leaf

## Method

1. Cook the carrot and shallot in the butter until softened and lightly
   coloured.
2. Add the vinegar and wine with the thyme and bay. Reduce until nearly dry.
3. Pour in the espagnole and simmer gently 25 minutes.
4. Add the crushed peppercorns and steep 8 to 10 minutes off the heat.
5. Strain through a fine sieve.

## Notes

- The peppercorns go in near the end. Boiled for the whole cook they turn harsh
  and dominate everything.
- Crush them coarsely. Ground pepper makes the sauce gritty and cloudy.
