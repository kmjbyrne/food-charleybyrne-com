One of the oldest sauces in the French repertoire. Onion softened in butter,
white wine reduced over it, espagnole added, mustard stirred in at the end.

## Ingredients

### Added to the base

- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 30g butter
- 150ml dry white wine
- 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
- 1 tsp sugar, optional

## Method

1. Cook the onion gently in the butter until soft and pale gold.
2. Add the wine and reduce until nearly dry.
3. Pour in the espagnole and simmer 15 minutes.
4. Off the heat, stir in the mustard.
5. Taste. A pinch of sugar balances it if the wine was sharp.

## Notes

- Mustard goes in off the heat. Boiled, it turns bitter and loses its lift.
- Add chopped gherkins and it becomes sauce charcutière.
