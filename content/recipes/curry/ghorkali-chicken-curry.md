The chicken version of the <nuxt-link to="./ghorkali-lamb-curry">Ghorkali Lamb
Special</nuxt-link>. Chicken thigh does not need a pressure cooker, so this
stays in an open pan throughout. The structure is the same: a cooked backbone,
then a fresh finish that never gets boiled.

## Ingredients

### Meat

- 800g boneless chicken thigh, cut in chunks

### Cooked Base

- 3 tbsp mustard oil
- 1 tsp jimbu (optional)
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 3 medium onions, roughly chopped
- 6 garlic cloves
- 30g ginger, roughly chopped
- 150ml water, for blending

### Dry Spices

- 1/2 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- 1 1/4 tsp salt

### Raw Green Paste

- 40g fresh coriander, leaves and stems
- 3 green chillies
- 2 garlic cloves
- 10g ginger

### Finish

- 50g cashew nuts, soaked
- 60ml double cream
- 1/2 tsp timur, toasted and ground
- 1 lemon, juiced

## Method

### Prep the Pastes

1. Cover the cashews with just-boiled water and leave them to soften while you
   do everything else.
2. Blend the fresh coriander, green chillies, 2 garlic cloves and 10g ginger
   with a splash of water into a loose, bright paste. Set aside. This is stirred
   in raw at the very end.

### Build the Base

1. Heat the mustard oil in a wide pan until it shimmers and the raw pungency
   burns off. Take it off the heat for 2 minutes so you do not scorch the
   aromatics.
2. Back on medium, add the jimbu and cumin seeds and let them sizzle 15-20
   seconds until fragrant.
3. Add the onions, 6 garlic cloves and 30g ginger. Fry, stirring, until deep
   golden, past the point that feels comfortable. With no tomato, this browning
   is the body and colour of the dish.
4. Tip the browned mixture into a blender with the 150ml water and blitz smooth.
   Return it to the pan.
5. Stir in the turmeric, ground cumin, ground coriander and salt, then add the
   chicken. Simmer uncovered 15-20 minutes, stirring now and then, until the
   chicken is cooked through and the sauce has thickened and gone glossy.

### Finish Off the Heat

1. Blend the soaked cashews smooth with a splash of their water and stir in,
   followed by the cream. Turn the heat low, stir through the reserved green
   paste and the timur, and warm 1-2 minutes only. Do not boil it green.
2. Off the heat, stir in the lemon juice. Taste for salt and lemon, and serve.

## Notes

- No timur? Use Sichuan peppercorn. It is the same genus, with the same
  citrus-numbing tingle and slightly less floral. Use the same amount, toasted
  and ground. Toasted coriander seed with lemon zest gives you the citrus but no
  tingle. Black pepper does not work here.
- Soak the cashews whole, then blend them to a paste before they go in. Cream
  adds richness. Cashew paste adds structure.
- Split the garlic and ginger between the cooked base and the raw green paste.
  Keep them in separate piles while you prep.
- Chicken breast works, but it goes dry. Add it at step 7 for the last 10
  minutes only.
- For lamb, prawns or anything else, see the
  <nuxt-link to="./ghorkali-lamb-curry">lamb version</nuxt-link>.
