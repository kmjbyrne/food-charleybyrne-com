## Ingredients

### Dough

- 1 cup (115g) grated mozzarella
- 1/2 cup (48g) ground almond flour
- 3 tbsp cream cheese
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 large egg
- 1 tsp baking powder

### Spread

- 3 garlic cloves minced
- 2 tbsp butter
- 1 tbsp dried parsley
- 1/4 tsp thyme or rosemary (optional)
- 1 tbsp grated parmesan
