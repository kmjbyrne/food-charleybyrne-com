## Ingredients

- 2 tbsp butter
- 1 1/2 tsp glucomannan
- 2 garlic cloves
- 1 large onion
- 1 large carrot
- 1/2 cup chopped celery
- 600ml (2 1/2 cup) fish stock water
- 350ml (1 1/2 cup) double cream
- 4 slices smokey streaky bacon
- Seasonings
  - 1/2 tsp thyme
  - 1 tsp sea salt
  - 3/4 tsp ground black pepper
  - Pinch of mace
  - Pinch of cayenne pepper (optional)
- 500g - 750g fish pie mix / mixed seafood
- 150g shellfish (prawns at least)

## Notes

The cream is not just for richness. Milk has about 4.7g of carbohydrate per
100ml from lactose, which would add roughly 13g of sugars to the pot. Double
cream has 2.6g per 100ml. Using cream leaves room for the onions and carrots,
which carry their own natural sugars, so the aromatics stay in.
