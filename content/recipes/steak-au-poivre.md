The sauce is the long part and it is a recipe in its own right, so make it first
or the day before. The steak itself takes ten minutes.

## Ingredients

### Steak

- 2 sirloin or fillet steaks, 200g each, at room temperature
- 2 tbsp black peppercorns, coarsely crushed
- 1 tbsp neutral oil
- 30g butter
- Flaky salt

### To finish

- 2 tbsp brandy or cognac
- 100ml double cream, optional
- 200ml sauce poivrade, warm

## Method

1. Pat the steaks dry. Press the crushed peppercorns firmly into both sides and
   salt them just before they hit the pan.
2. Heat the oil in a heavy pan until it shimmers. Sear the steaks 2 to 3 minutes
   a side for medium rare, adding the butter for the last minute and basting.
3. Rest the steaks on a warm plate while you finish the sauce.
4. Pour off the excess fat. Off the heat, add the brandy and let it flame or
   simmer off.
5. Add the warm poivrade and any resting juices. Simmer 2 minutes.
6. Stir in the cream if you want it softer, and check the seasoning.
7. Spoon over the rested steaks.

## Notes

- Salt just before cooking or a good while ahead. In between, it draws moisture
  to the surface and you lose the crust.
- Resting is not optional. Five minutes minimum, and the juices go into the
  sauce.
- Without the poivrade made in advance, the honest version is brandy, stock and
  cream reduced in the pan. It is a different, lighter sauce.
