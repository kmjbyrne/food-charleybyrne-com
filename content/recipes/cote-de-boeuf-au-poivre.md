Two things happen on the day. The steak goes in the bath in the morning, then the sear and the sauce take about fifteen minutes between them.

The glace de viande is a prerequisite rather than a step, made well in advance. I freeze mine in cubes so there is always some to hand.

## Ingredients

### Steak

- 1 côte de boeuf, 800g to 1kg, bone in
- 1 tbsp neutral oil, high smoke point
- 30g butter
- Flaky salt
- Black pepper

### Sauce

:sub-recipe{path="/recipes/sauces/pepper-sauce" section="Ingredients" label="Pepper sauce"}

## Method

### Sous Vide

1. Season the côte de boeuf with salt only. Pepper burns in the sear.
2. Bag it and cook at 54°C for 3 to 4 hours for medium rare. Longer is fine up to about 6 hours.
3. Take it out and dry it thoroughly. Wet meat steams instead of searing, and the crust is the point.

### Sear

1. Get a heavy pan to smoking with the oil.
2. Sear 60 to 90 seconds a side, standing it on the fat edge for another minute.
3. Add the butter in the last 30 seconds and baste hard.
4. Move it to a board to rest while you make the sauce.

### Sauce and Serve

1. Make the pepper sauce in the same pan, following its method.
2. Add any juices from the resting board to the sauce.
3. Carve off the bone against the grain and spoon the sauce over.

## Notes

- Dry the meat properly after the bath. This is the single thing that decides whether you get a crust.
- The sear is short because the meat is already cooked through. You are building colour, not temperature.
- Rest it while the sauce comes together, so the timing works out on its own.
