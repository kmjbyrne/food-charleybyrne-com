Same method as the beef glace, two hours instead of three. Chicken bones are more delicate and give up their gelatin faster, so a longer cook turns the stock cloudy and the flavour flat.

## Ingredients

- 2kg chicken bones, backs, wings and carcasses
- 2 onions, halved
- 2 carrots, roughly chopped
- 2 sticks celery, roughly chopped
- 2 tbsp white wine vinegar
- 2 bay leaves, few sprigs thyme, handful parsley stalks
- 1 tbsp white peppercorns
- Water to cover

## Method

1. Roast the bones at 220°C for 30 minutes if you want a brown stock. Skip this entirely for a white stock to use in a velouté.
2. Add the vegetables for the last 15 minutes if roasting.
3. Tip everything into the pressure cooker. Deglaze the tray with the vinegar and add that too.
4. Add the herbs and peppercorns, cover with water, and pressure cook 2 hours.
5. Strain through a fine sieve, then again through muslin. Chill overnight and lift off the fat.
6. Reduce until syrupy and it coats a spoon heavily.
7. Freeze in an ice cube tray.

## Notes

- Two hours, not three. Chicken bones past that point give a cloudy stock with a dull, boiled flavour.
- White peppercorns rather than black, since black specks show in a pale stock.
- Skip the roasting for a white stock. That is what velouté wants, and browning it makes the sauce beige.
- Do not salt it. This reduces heavily, so seasoning now makes it inedible later.
